# Launch kit — Occasion Speech Writer

Ready-to-use copy and a sequencing plan for getting the first users once the
site is live (see `README.md` for the setup steps that come first). Nothing
here needs a design tool — screenshots/GIFs can be captured directly from
the live site once it's deployed.

## Sequencing

1. **Directory submissions** — no lead time, do these first.
2. **Reddit posts** — from your own account (needs to not look like a brand-new/bot account).
3. **Product Hunt** — only once you have an active maker profile (ideally
   ~30 days of prior activity); otherwise this is a week 3-4+ lever, not a
   day-one one. Skip it entirely at first if you don't already use Product Hunt.
4. **SEO landing pages** — already live in the codebase, no extra work; payoff is slow (months).

## 1. Directory submission kit

Submit to (free, in priority order): **There's An AI For That** (taaft.com),
**Toolify.ai**, **Smol Launch**, **AI Hunter**, **Best AI Brands**. Skip
Futurepedia — it's paid-submission only now, not worth it pre-revenue.

Use this exact kit for all of them (5-6 complete listings beat 20 thin ones):

- **Name**: Occasion Speech Writer
- **Tagline** (≤60 chars): Never stare at a blank page before a wedding speech again
- **One-liner**: Answer a few quick questions and get a personalized best man, maid of honor, wedding toast, or retirement speech in under a minute — free preview, no signup.
- **Description** (~150 words):
  > Occasion Speech Writer turns a few quick details — who it's for, your
  > relationship, a couple of memories, the tone you want — into a
  > personalized speech in under a minute. Built for best man and maid of
  > honor speeches, wedding toasts, and retirement send-offs. Try it free —
  > you get a real preview, not a teaser — and unlock the full, ready-to-read
  > speech for $8.99 with instant PDF download. No account required.
- **Category tags**: writing tools, wedding, productivity, text generation
- **URL**: (your live Vercel URL once deployed)

## 2. Reddit post draft

Candidates: r/weddingplanning, r/AskMen, r/PublicSpeaking, r/Toastmasters —
**check each subreddit's current self-promotion rules right before posting**;
they vary and change. Lead with the free tool as something helpful, disclose
you built it if asked, don't hide the paid unlock.

**Title**: I built a free tool that writes best man/wedding speeches from a
few quick questions — thought this sub might find it useful

**Body**:
> I've watched a few friends panic about writing a best man/maid of honor
> speech, so I put together a small tool: answer a few questions about the
> couple/person and your relationship to them, and it writes a full speech.
> The preview is free to try — no signup. If you want the full text and a
> PDF, it's a small one-time unlock.
>
> Link: (your live URL)
>
> Would love feedback, especially if the tone feels off for any occasion.

## 3. Product Hunt draft (only once you have an active account)

- **Tagline** (≤60 chars): Write a speech worth giving, in under a minute
- **Best launch days**: Tuesday/Wednesday for max raw traffic, or
  Friday-Sunday for an easier "#1 Product of the Day" badge with less
  competition.
- **Maker comment draft**:
  > Hey everyone — I built this after staring at a blank page before a
  > friend's wedding and realizing how much of the stress was just "where do
  > I even start." Answer a few questions about the occasion and the person,
  > and it writes a full speech — free to preview, small one-time unlock for
  > the full text and PDF. Would love your feedback, especially on tone and
  > which occasions to add next.
- **Assets needed**: 8-12 screenshots/GIFs of the actual flow (form →
  preview → unlock → download) — capture these from the live site once
  deployed.
